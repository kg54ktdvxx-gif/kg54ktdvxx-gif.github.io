VaultClipboard Support

Clipboard history for the Mac, with sensitive items encrypted behind Touch ID.

Contact

Email busiest-21.qualm@icloud.com with your macOS version, the app version (in Preferences › About) and what happened. Feature ideas are welcome too.

Where your clipboard history is kept

Only on your Mac. VaultClipboard makes no network requests and has no accounts, analytics or crash reporting. See the privacy policy.

Encrypted items

Encrypted items are locked with a key kept in your macOS Keychain. Opening one asks for Touch ID or your Mac password.

Requirements

macOS 26.1 or later.
