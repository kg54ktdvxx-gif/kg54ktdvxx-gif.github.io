Privacy Policy — VaultClipboard

Last updated: 2 November 2026 (contact details changed; nothing else)

Overview

VaultClipboard is a clipboard manager for macOS. Your privacy is fundamental to how the app is built.

Data Collection

VaultClipboard does not collect, transmit, or share any data. Period.

  • No analytics
  • No telemetry
  • No crash reporting
  • No network requests
  • No user accounts

Data Storage

All clipboard history is stored locally on your Mac using Core Data. Nothing leaves your device.

  • Text, images, links, and colours you copy are saved to a local database
  • Source app information (name and icon) is stored alongside each item
  • If you enable encryption, items are encrypted at rest using AES-256 (Apple CryptoKit) with a key stored in your macOS Keychain

Encryption

When you encrypt a clipboard item:

  • The item is encrypted using AES-256-GCM via Apple's CryptoKit framework
  • The encryption key is stored in your macOS Keychain, protected by your system credentials
  • Decryption requires biometric authentication (Touch ID) or your macOS password
  • Encryption and decryption happen entirely on your device

Third-Party Services

VaultClipboard uses no third-party services, SDKs, or frameworks beyond Apple's standard macOS frameworks.

Data Deletion

You can delete individual clipboard items or clear your entire history at any time from within the app. Deleted data is permanently removed from the local database.

Children's Privacy

VaultClipboard does not collect any data from anyone, including children.
