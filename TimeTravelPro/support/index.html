Contact us: email busiest-21.qualm@icloud.com. Please include your device, its iOS or macOS version and the app version (shown in Settings on iPhone and in About on Mac).

Common questions

How do I share my team's cities?

On iPhone, open the World Clock, tap the ⋯ button and choose Share Team Link. Teammates who tap the link can preview the cities and add them in one step. The link also includes an App Store link for anyone who doesn't have the app yet.

A team link doesn't open the app

Make sure Time Travel Pro is installed and up to date: team links open the app from version 2.1 on.

How do I switch between 12-hour and 24-hour time?

Go to Settings, then Time Format. System follows your device's own 12/24-hour setting.

Why don't I see my meetings?

In Settings, under Calendar Integration, tap Grant Access. If you turned access off earlier, allow full calendar access for Time Travel Pro in your device's Privacy & Security settings.

How are meeting times chosen?

The meeting finder looks for times inside everyone's work hours. Set yours in Settings, under Work Hours; a city can also have its own hours, and work hours can run overnight.

Do my cities sync between devices?

Yes, when you're signed in to iCloud on each device. Your cities, settings and teammates sync automatically; teammates' photos stay on the device that added them.

How do I add a widget?

On iPhone, touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose Time Travel Pro. On Mac, Control-click the desktop and choose Edit Widgets.
