Time Travel Pro Support Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 November 2026. Applies to Time Travel Pro for iPhone and Mac.

In short: Time Travel Pro doesn't collect any data about you. There are no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking, and we don't run any servers.

Data collection

Time Travel Pro stores everything on your device: your cities, settings, work hours and the teammates you add. We don't collect analytics, usage data or any form of tracking information, and nothing is sent to us.

iCloud sync

If you're signed in to iCloud, your cities, settings and teammates (their names, roles and cities) sync between your devices through Apple's iCloud. Teammates' photos stay on the device that added them. Apple handles the storage and encryption, and we can't see this data. Signing out of iCloud stops the sync.

Calendar access

Calendar access is optional. If you allow it, the app reads your calendars on your device to show upcoming meetings and to create the events you ask for. Your calendar data is never uploaded, shared or stored anywhere else. You can turn access off at any time in your device's privacy settings.

Photos

Time Travel Pro only adds to your photo library: when you share a card and choose Save Image, the card is saved there. The app can't see or read your photos.

Sharing

Share cards, team links and calendar files contain only what you choose to share, such as cities and work hours. A team link carries that information inside the link itself; nothing is uploaded. They're sent only through the app or service you pick.

Third parties

Time Travel Pro doesn't share any data with third parties. It contains no advertising, analytics or other third-party services.

Children

Time Travel Pro doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
