Common questions

Voice notes aren't transcribing. Check that Flash Note Pro has Microphone and Speech Recognition access in Settings › Privacy & Security.

Where are my notes stored? Only on your device. If you delete the app, its notes are deleted with it, so export anything you want to keep first.
